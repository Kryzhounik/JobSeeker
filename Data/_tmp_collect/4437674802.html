About the job

Senior Dynamics 365 Commerce Consultant (POS)


Location: Fully Remote (Europe)

Contract: 6–12 Months

Rate: €500–€650 per day

Start: ASAP


About the Role


We are partnering with a leading international retail organisation undergoing a significant digital transformation and are looking for an experienced Senior Microsoft Dynamics 365 Commerce Consultant with deep expertise in Point of Sale (POS) implementations.

This is a fully remote contract offering the opportunity to work on a large-scale D365 Commerce programme, delivering enhancements and new functionality across retail operations.


Key Responsibilities
  • Lead the design and implementation of Microsoft Dynamics 365 Commerce solutions.
  • Configure, customise and optimise D365 Commerce, with a strong focus on Store Commerce / Modern POS.
  • Gather business requirements and translate them into scalable technical solutions.
  • Work closely with business stakeholders, architects and development teams throughout the project lifecycle.
  • Support integration with Finance & Operations and other retail systems.
  • Troubleshoot complex POS issues and provide technical guidance to project teams.
  • Participate in solution design workshops and contribute to best practice across the programme.
  • Support testing, deployment and post-go-live activities.


Essential Skills & Experience
  • Extensive experience delivering Microsoft Dynamics 365 Commerce solutions.
  • Strong hands-on experience with Point of Sale (POS) / Store Commerce.
  • Proven track record working on large retail transformation programmes.
  • Strong understanding of:
  • D365 Commerce architecture
  • Retail operations
  • Product, pricing and promotions
  • Inventory and store management
  • Customer and loyalty functionality
  • Experience integrating D365 Commerce with Finance & Operations.
  • Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills.
  • Ability to work independently within a fully remote, international team.


Desirable
  • Experience with Azure DevOps.
  • Knowledge of Power Platform.
  • Experience working within Agile delivery environments.
  • Microsoft Dynamics 365 certifications.
  • Previous experience within enterprise retail organisations.


What's on Offer
  • €500–€600 per day
  • Fully remote
  • 6–12 month contract with strong likelihood of extension
  • Opportunity to join a market-leading retail business on a major Dynamics 365 Commerce programme
  • Collaborative international team with a modern technology stack
