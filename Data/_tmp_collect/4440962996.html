Role Overview:

We are hiring for one of our clients, seeking a Computer Science Expert (PhD) to work on a contract basis. • Develop and optimize algorithms for large-scale data processing and machine learning applications.

• Design and implement scalable software systems to meet performance and reliability requirements.

• Conduct in-depth research to advance the state-of-the-art in computer science, including theoretical and applied contributions.

• Collaborate with engineering teams to translate research findings into production-ready solutions.

• Evaluate emerging technologies and methodologies to enhance computational efficiency and system architecture.


Required Skills & Qualifications:

• PhD in Computer Science or a closely related field, with a strong academic or industry research background.

• Expertise in algorithm design, complexity analysis, and computational theory.

• Proficiency in programming languages such as Python, C++, or Java, with experience in low-level optimization.

• Experience with distributed computing frameworks, parallel processing, or high-performance computing environments.

• Familiarity with machine learning frameworks, data structures, and optimization techniques for real-world applications.
