About the job

About Us

Cedacri International is part of ION Group, a community of visionary innovators, dedicated to providing pioneering software and consultancy services to financial institutions, trading firms, central banks, governments, and corporations around the world. We strive to simplify the way people work. We do that by providing workflow and process automation software, as well as providing real-time data and business intelligence to help people make better decisions. We are 13,000+ employees, 60+ global offices, and over 4,800+ customers worldwide.

Your Role

We're in search of Junior and Mid-Level RPA Developers (UiPath) ready to innovate and explore what's possible in the world of intelligent automation. In this role, you'll work cross-functionally with business operations and analysts to design, develop, and optimize automation workflows using UiPath. You will also support initiatives related to the management, deployment, and maintenance of RPA projects and contribute to digital transformation within the organization.

RPA Developer - Junior Level

Responsibilities

Analyze business requirements and identify automation opportunities.

Design, develop, and test RPA workflows using UiPath Studio.

Deploy and monitor bots via UiPath Orchestrator.

Debug and resolve issues to ensure flawless execution of automations.

Provide post-deployment support and continuous improvement.

Create and maintain technical and end-user documentation.

Ensure quality and performance through thorough QA processes.

Collaborate with team members for integration and delivery.

Requirements

Programming knowledge in one or more: C#, VB.NET, Python, JavaScript, Java, or C/C++.

Understanding of RPA concepts, development lifecycle, and governance.

Strong grasp of workflow logic, UI selectors, and exception handling.

Familiarity with SQL or NoSQL databases for data-driven automation.

Experience with REST/SOAP APIs for system integration.

Basic knowledge of scripting and web technologies (HTML, JavaScript).

Strong attention to detail, analytical mindset, and good communication skills.

What We Offer

A 3-month paid training period.

Work in a dynamic and collaborative team.

Italian language lessons and continuous professional development.

Real opportunities for career advancement in a growing, international environment.

Location:

Chisinau, Republic of Moldova
